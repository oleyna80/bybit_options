# Agent Reports

This folder contains **historical role-scoped research/review evidence** produced
under the pre-migration Orchestrator protocol.

It is not a current role registry or authority source. Current role authority and
routing live in `governance/authority.md` and `.agent/ROSTER.md`; current SDLC
evidence belongs under `docs/reports/` when created by an active Work Block.

Historical reports may contain old role names (`Researcher`, `QA`) and old paths
such as `.agent/workflow/...` (singular). Preserve those records as evidence of
what was assessed at the time; do not silently reinterpret them as current paths
or current completion state.

Before relying on a historical `PASSED`, `APPROVED`, or “ready” statement, bind
it to the referenced revision/files and verify that the evidence still exists.
Otherwise current applicability is `UNKNOWN`.
